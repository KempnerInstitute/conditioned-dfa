# nDFA claim map before joint test evaluation

This document maps the existing frozen tests to manuscript claims. It is written after reviewing development results and training-status information, before opening the joint confirmation's official-test results. It is a pre-test interpretation record, not a claim that this document preceded development. Historical exposure to the standard CIFAR/MNIST/Fashion test sets remains disclosed. It adds no primary tests, changes no threshold and selects no new model, seed, setting, checkpoint or horizon.

The governing definitions remain `integrated_round_v1/protocol.md`, `plan.json`, the frozen `source/scripts/ndfa_strengthening_20260925/integrated_control.py`, and the prospectively amended `joint_confirmation.json`. This map and its timestamp/hash receipt are preserved separately, leaving those scientific definitions intact.

## Rules shared by the primary claims

There are 21 primary tests. A positive result requires the prescribed positive mean and two-sided paired-test Holm-adjusted p < 0.05 across all 21 tests. Accuracy differences are percentage points; positive loss differences mean lower cross-entropy. Display all seed-level contrasts and the prescribed individual paired t intervals. Those intervals are not simultaneous familywise intervals.

An unavailable comparison receives p = 1 under the frozen analysis. Missing or interrupted data are an availability problem, not evidence of a scientific null. A failed positive test does not establish equivalence or prove that the effect is absent. A significant effect in the opposite direction must be reported as such. Keep all planned conditions and the four numerical development failures visible. Do not replace the selected learning rate or benchmark with one that looks better after evaluation.

All ten declared seed pairs are required for a primary contrast. Measured-work comparisons additionally require uninterrupted runs. Fixed-update geometry and timing comparisons retain their permitted checkpoint-resumption policy. Infrastructure problems must retain their provenance and must not be repaired according to accuracy or loss outcomes.

## Secondary evidence and boundaries

The temporary-activity efficiency criterion is unchanged: the one-sided 95% lower bound for early-minus-full final accuracy must exceed -0.5 points, and the mean paired learning-work ratio must be at most 0.8, with the required uninterrupted work measurements. Passing supports the specified fixed-epoch recipe. It does not guarantee noninferiority under constant learning rates, every horizon or every optimizer.

The four clean/label-noise equivalence checks use the prescribed individual 90% paired intervals inside +/-1 accuracy point. Treat these as secondary equivalence checks, not a familywise assertion of no effect. Nonsignificance of a difference is insufficient for equivalence.

The synthetic BP/DFA-by-FOOF comparison, shared-checkpoint branches, nuisance-variation probes, MNIST E result, FD comparisons, remaining geometry controls, final checkpoints in work-budget studies and all development sweeps retain their declared secondary or development roles. In particular, the synthetic FOOF interaction is not one of the 21 primary tests. A favorable synthetic comparison must not be promoted into a newly preregistered primary success. Existing branch results show that late activation can help both credit rules; they do not establish inability to recover.

Selected development values may be optimistic because settings were chosen using the same validation data on which those values are reported. There is no rule that every effect must shrink in confirmation. Report the actual confirmation estimates and uncertainty without changing the protocol if their ordering differs.

## Hardware deviation

The entire 420-run benchmark confirmation moved from H200 to RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell after development and before any benchmark confirmation run began. All selected settings, seeds, precision, selection rules and the nominal 60-second learning-work budget were retained; every comparison arm moved together. The original manifest and the prospective amendment are preserved in `scheduler/rtx_benchmark_amendment_20260926T063130Z/`.

This is a deviation from the planned confirmation hardware. Equal seconds on the two accelerators do not imply equal updates, arithmetic work, training trajectory or effective optimization horizon. Report the actual per-method update counts and estimated linear-algebra FLOPs. The RTX cohort tests transfer of the selected recipe under its RTX time budget; it is not an exact replication of the H200 training horizon. Within-cohort RTX comparisons remain hardware matched. Do not assume that early selected checkpoints make the deviation inconsequential, and do not present an interim aggregate update ratio as a universal hardware speedup.

Suggested methods disclosure: “We selected benchmark settings on H200 GPUs. Before any confirmation run began, queue constraints led us to move the full benchmark cohort, including every baseline, to RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPUs. We retained the selected settings and learning-work budget and recorded this prospective hardware deviation. Since equal time budgets can yield different numbers of updates, we report accelerator-specific learning time, update counts and estimated linear-algebra work.”

## Conditional follow-up if E passes

No additional E experiment is launched before the current joint decision. If all five E criteria pass and the authors pursue DFA specificity, first freeze a small separate BP/DFA-by-A/early-K factorial, with matched operator definitions, update-norm conventions, loss, architecture, windows, constant-rate schedules and within-study hardware. Use independent seeds, a declared selection policy and the existing accuracy-and-loss requirements. Prespecify the direct interaction `(K - A)_DFA - (K - A)_BP`; significance in DFA and nonsignificance in BP is not evidence that the effects differ. A claim of negligible BP benefit also needs an equivalence bound, not merely a nonsignificant test.

Name these implementations according to the factors they actually use. Our gated empirical error moments, norm matching and layer scope do not automatically constitute standard Fisher KFAC. A faithful KFAC-BP versus FOOF-BP comparison could be an additional optimizer comparison, but it cannot substitute for the matched credit-rule interaction. The new study would be a conditional follow-up motivated by the current confirmation, not part of its original primary family.

## Paper and venue decision

Keep the main figures focused on practical performance and loss, covariance-versus-mean controls, timing/shared-state interventions, and the benchmark predictions. Preserve relevant failed predictions rather than accumulating every exploratory experiment in the supplement. Choose the title and abstract after the complete decision; a passed E gate would make error geometry eligible for a conditional claim, not automatically the central contribution.

A credible distinctive mechanism or strong broader practical contribution would justify reassessing a conference target. A carefully scoped paper about when conditioning helps random-feedback learning can also be useful. Venue choice is a judgment about the final evidence and presentation, not a deterministic consequence of E passing or failing, and no outcome guarantees acceptance.
